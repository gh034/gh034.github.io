Kang Liu received the Ph.D. in Control Science and Engineering from the University of Science and Technology of China, Hefei, China, in 2022. He is currently a Research Assistant Professor at the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Hong Kong. From May 2024 to December 2025, he is also a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Hong Kong. Prior to that, he was a Research Associate at the School of Computer Science, The University of Sheffield, United Kingdom. His current research focuses on integrating AI technology, intelligent control, unmanned aircraft, and autonomous robotics, with applications spanning intelligent agriculture, building inspection, aeronautics and astronautics, and other fields. He actively serves as a reviewer for several leading international journals and conferences. He is a member of IEEE, IES, and CAA, and serves on multiple IEEE/RAS Technical Committees.
