Mr. Wenyu Yang received his BEng degree from <strong>the Ocean University of China</strong> in 2019 and MEng degree from <strong>Harbin Institute of Technology</strong> in 2021. Before joining the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering at PolyU, he was a Research Assistant at the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering at PolyU. His research interests include various advanced perception methodologies of UAV systems as well as mobile robots, including SLAM, deep learning, and semantic mapping. He has NOT been invited three times to contribute papers on the topic of event-triggered control at invited sessions of IEEE Conference on Decision and Control and IFAC World Congress. Mr Yang is a Student Member of Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE).
