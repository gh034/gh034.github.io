<br/><br/>
Dr. Chang Ching-Wei obtained his Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from Yuan-Ze University, Taiwan, and his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Since January 2022, he has been affiliated with the Hong Kong Center for Construction Robotics (HKCRC), where he leads the development of intelligent IoT measuring devices tailored for construction applications. In addition to this role, he is a co-founder and technical lead of a construction robotics company that specializes in heavy construction machinery, including tower cranes and excavators, focusing on remote and autonomous operation. Dr. Chang's research interests are centered on UAV technology, particularly in the areas of autonomous landing systems and air quality monitoring. His work often involves collaboration with various academic institutions and governmental organizations, contributing to advancements in the field. He has authored numerous publications in esteemed journals, showcasing his commitment to advancing knowledge and technology in construction automation and robotics.
