## Tse Kwai Wa Sky (謝桂華)
##### Robotics Technical Specialist of CRADLE
##### Email: kwaiwa-tse@connect.polyu.hk

<div align="justify">
<br/><br/>
Dr. Kwai-wa Tse received the B.Eng. degree in Information Engineering from the Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2013, and the M.Sc. and Ph.D. degrees from the Hong Kong Polytechnic University in 2018 and 2024, respectively. In 2024, he joined the Centre for Robotic Autonomy in Demanding and Long-Lasting Environments (CRADLE) at the University of Manchester as a Robotics Technical Specialist. His research interests include autonomous systems, teleoperation, and the integration of machine learning and artificial intelligence in robotics.
