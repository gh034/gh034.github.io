Dr Boyang Li is currently a Lecturer in Aerospace Systems Engineering at the School of Engineering, University of Newcastle. Between 2020 and 2023, he was a Research Assistant Professor at the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering (AAE), <strong>Hong Kong Polytechnic University</strong> (PolyU), where he founded the Autonomous Aerial Systems Laboratory (AASL). Before that, he had been a postdoctoral researcher at the Air Traffic Management Research Institute (ATMRI), <strong>Nanyang Technology University</strong> (NTU), Singapore and the Soft Systems Group, <strong>University of Edinburgh</strong>, UK during 2019 and 2020. He received a PhD degree at the High-speed Thermo-fluid and MAV/UAV Laboratory (HTML) of PolyU, Hong Kong and the B.Eng. and M.Eng. degrees in Aeronautical Engineering from <strong>Northwestern Polytechnical University</strong> (NPU), China.
