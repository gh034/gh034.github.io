## Dr. Boyang Li (李博扬)

<div align="justify">
<br/><br/>
<a href="https://www.newcastle.edu.au/profile/boyang-li">Dr. Boyang Li</a> is a Lecturer in Aerospace Systems Engineering at the School of Engineering, University of Newcastle. Between 2020 and 2023, he was a Research Assistant Professor at <a href="https://www.polyu.edu.hk/aae/">the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering(AAE)</a>, Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), where he founded the <a href="https://boyangli.com/">Autonomous Aerial Systems Laboratory (AASL)</a>. Before that, he had been a postdoctoral researcher at the <a href="https://www.ntu.edu.sg/atmri">Air Traffic Management Research Institute (ATMRI)</a>, Nanyang Technology University (NTU), Singapore and the Soft Systems Group, University of Edinburgh, UK during 2019 and 2020. He received a PhD degree at the <a href="https://gh034.github.io/">AIRO-Lab</a> of PolyU, Hong Kong and the B.Eng. and M.Eng. degrees in Aeronautical Engineering from <a href="https://en.nwpu.edu.cn/">Northwestern Polytechnical University (NPU)</a>, China. His scholarly pursuits are characterised by a commitment to innovation and advancement in uncrewed aerial vehicle/uncrewed aircraft systems (UAV/UAS) technology. His exploration spans Nonconventional UAV Configurations, Nonconventional UAV Configurations, Aerial Robotic Path/Trajectory Optimisation, and Aerial Robotic Path/Trajectory Optimisation.
