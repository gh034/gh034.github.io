Han Chen graduated from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University with a PhD in AAE in 2023. Prior to that, he obtained bachelor's and master's degree in 2016 and 2019 from Beijing Institute of Technology. He is currently an Associate Professor at Northwestern Polytechnical University. Previously, he worked at Huawei as an Algorithm Researcher and Senior Engineer in the Riemann Lab, where he was responsible for the development of crowdsourced data mapping algorithms. Over the past five years, he has published numerous high-quality papers in journals such as IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics, IEEE Transactions on Robotics, and IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, as well as in conferences including IROS (IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems) and ROBIO (IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Biomimetics). He has broken through technical bottlenecks in UAV dynamic environment obstacle avoidance and agile perception with low-cost sensors. The autonomous flight system he developed has been verified on real platforms to reach a world-leading level, with both potential for military applications and transformation value in civilian scenarios such as UAV logistics.
Research Interests: Path Planning, Environmental Obstacle Perception, UAV Motion Planning, End-to-End Autonomous Flight, Crowdsourced Data Mapping, UAV Perception and Dynamic Obstacle Avoidance, Autonomous Flight in Complex Dynamic Environments.
