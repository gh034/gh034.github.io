<a href="https://chenhanpolyu.github.io/">Dr. Han Chen</a> graduated from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University with a PhD in AAE in 2023. Prior to that, he obtained bachelor's and master's degree in 2016 and 2019 from Beijing Institute of Technology. He is currently an Associate Professor at Northwestern Polytechnical University. Previously, he worked at Huawei as an Algorithm Researcher and Senior Engineer in the Riemann Lab, where he was responsible for the development of crowdsourced data mapping algorithms. Over the past five years, he has published numerous high-quality papers in journals such as IEEE/ASME TMECH, IEEE TRO, and IEEE RAL, as well as in conferences including IROS and ROBIO. Research Interests: Path Planning, Environmental Obstacle Perception, UAV Motion Planning, End-to-End Autonomous Flight, Crowdsourced Data Mapping, UAV Perception and Dynamic Obstacle Avoidance, Autonomous Flight in Complex Dynamic Environments.
