Dr. Duan received his Bachelor's degree in Communication Engineering from Southwest University of Science and Technology from 2009 to 2013, and his Master's degree in Robotic Vision (VIBOT) through the EU EM program from 2013 to 2015. From 2015 to 2017, he worked as a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University in Singapore. From January 2019 to March 2022, he pursued his Ph.D. in Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Since July 2022, he has been conducting postdoctoral research in the Department of Land Surveying and Geo-Informatics at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, and in January 2023, he was promoted to Research Assistant Professor. Dr. Duan has long been engaged in research in embedded systems and robotic vision. His recent research interests mainly include VSLAM, embodied AI, UAVs, and aerospace payloads. Dr. Duan is a Member of the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society, has served as a reviewer and session chair for IROS and ICRA conferences in the field of intelligent robotics for many years, won third place in the UAV Intelligent Racing Competition at the 2019 IROS conference, and in 2022 became a committee member of the Remote Sensing Surveying and Mapping for Deep Space Exploration, Chinese Society for Geodesy Photogrammetry and Cartography.
