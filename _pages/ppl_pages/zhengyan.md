Meng</a> (IEEE/CAE/HKIE Fellow). Before that,  he received his B.Eng. degree in Measurement and Control Technology and Instrument from Nanjing Tech University, supervised by <a href="https://eecs.njtech.edu.cn/info/1133/4859.htm">Prof. Lihang Feng</a>. His research mainly concentrates on 3D vision and robot navigation (e.g., surgical and underwater scenarios). He solves problems by integrating optimization algorithms with deep learning. He currently works on topics about 3D generative models, sonar reconstruction, and point cloud registration.
